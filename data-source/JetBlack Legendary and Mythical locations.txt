---Post-Game---

With the exception of Latias, Latios, Phione and Manaphy,
as well as the ones listed above,  all Legendaries are housed 
in the Relic Castle as "Obelisks" I was playing around with the
idea of having the Obelisks scattered all around Unova however 
I was running into major problems like freezes so I wanted to 
play it on the safe side. Besides, this is kinda like a "resting"
place for all the Legendary Spirits and it will be a nice little
challenge if you wanted to go in and catch them all in one sitting!
They are all encountered at Level 70.

#490 Manaphy
------------
Undella Town.
-Found as an egg between the two rocks south of town.
Does NOT require obtaining the God Stone.


In order to encounter all of the Gen 1-4 Legendary Pokemon (With the exception of Manaphy)
you will need to find the Enigma Stone in Giant's Chasm, take it to an NPC in Anville Town
and then talk to a Veteran NPC in Nimbasa
