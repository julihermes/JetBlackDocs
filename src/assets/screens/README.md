# Screenshots

The nine in-game screenshots shown on the home page, taken from the hack's
Hackdex listing (https://www.hackdex.app/hack/pokemon-jetblack) and committed
here so the site serves them itself rather than hotlinking someone else's CDN.

They are screenshots of Pokémon JetBlack by EstrethAthema. `jetblack-0.png` is
the title screen and is used as the hero; the other eight are the strip below it.

To refresh them, open the Hackdex page and re-download the `images.hackdex.app`
URLs in DOM order — the numeric suffix here is that order.
